UNDERCURRENT

A daily research radar.

Every morning it reads eight public sources, throws away what it has already seen, and posts one short digest of what actually changed.

It is not a news feed. It tracks themes across days, so the interesting part is not that something was published — it is that unrelated sources started pointing the same direction at once.

What it reads

Hacker News · arXiv · GitHub · Substack · Medium · daily.dev · Reddit · X

What it posts

  • Openings worth a look, and why now
  • Open research questions
  • Papers worth reading
  • Trends with the evidence behind them
  • Small builds that would test an idea

Every claim carries links to the sources it came from. Anything resting on a single source says so. Sections are left empty on quiet days rather than padded.
