A daily research radar.

Eight public sources, read every morning. One short digest of what actually changed.

↓

A radar, not a feed. Every morning it reads eight public sources, discards what it has already seen, and posts one short digest to Discord.

It runs unattended and costs nothing to operate. There is no app to open and no inbox to manage — the digest arrives in a channel.

Most tools tell you what was published. This one keeps memory across days and tracks themes over time, so the signal is not that something appeared — it is that unrelated sources started pointing the same direction at once.

Duplicates collapse before anything is counted. The same story arriving twice raises confidence without pretending to be two findings, and one loud source never outweighs two quiet independent ones.

Every claim links to its sources. Anything resting on a single source says so. Quiet days leave sections empty rather than padding them.
